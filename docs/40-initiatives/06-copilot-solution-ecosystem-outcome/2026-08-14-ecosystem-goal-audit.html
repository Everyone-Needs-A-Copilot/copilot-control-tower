The standard being audited

The goal is a human outcome, not a software inventory

The Initiative 05 retrospective defines the finish line as giving every person a trustworthy, company-aware, multidisciplinary solution team that turns real problems into working solutions without requiring technical expertise. The product SOUL makes democratization—not installation—the essence.

Simplest expression of the goal: a real person starts with a real problem, receives the right company and department expertise through the right specialists and tools, preserves the work and evidence, and never needs Pablo to wire the ecosystem together.

1

Start with a problem

No terminal, repository, manifest, agent roster, or infrastructure assembly.

2

Assemble the team

Protocol routes the right multidisciplinary specialists across Claude and Codex.

3

Apply real context

Organization, department, and personal inputs appropriately change methods and decisions.

4

Create and preserve

Bounded operational tools help; Task Copilot keeps evidence and continuation durable.

5

Keep it working

Shared improvements reach entitled people safely, while Control Tower reports verified truth.

Decisive missing outcomes

Why those wins do not add up to “done”

01

The candidate has not passed the protected release gates

Framework PR #66 and accounting PR #1 remain blocked for review; organization PR #1 is clean but unreviewed. All three have zero human reviews.

What that meansThe exact tested bytes are still candidate bytes. They have not been merged and published through the required protected path.
Why it mattersSelf-review cannot replace the independent human gate that the trust model requires. No merge, tag, or release may be manufactured around it.
02

Live conformance still says “fail”

The complete no-cache run reports six S0 organization-resolution blockers and 149 lower-severity failures. There are zero unknowns and zero regression-layer failures, but those facts do not turn failed live rows into passes.

What that meansThe checker is complete enough to tell the truth, and the truth is that the classified fleet is not yet in the required state.
Why it mattersOne hundred and one failed rows still declare expected_today: pass. A stable baseline only says the comparison did not identify a new delta; it is not ecosystem health.
03

Installed and wired have not become effective

TASK-284 remains bounded and in progress. TASK-285 has not run. The hosted billed live-model evaluation was skipped, so none of the required foundation-versus-layered cases has produced a real outcome artifact.

What that meansWe can show that context can be selected and delivered, but not yet that it changes company or accounting work in the intended way.
Why it mattersThe original risk was a technically impressive system that reliably delivers generic assistance. Only behavioral evidence closes that risk.
04

Pablo is still part of the rollout path

The 75-row fleet census is preparation with zero mutation authority. Fan-out, pre-fan-out security, and post-fan-out security have not run.

What that meansNo reviewed organization or department improvement has yet traveled once through the canonical path to the exact eligible projects.
Why it mattersThe ecosystem goal explicitly removes Pablo as the person who copies, connects, interprets, and repairs every project by hand.
05

The primary user has not proved the journey

TASK-303 has not proved the framework in an isolated clean environment, and TASK-290 has no non-technical participant artifact.

What that meansThere is no observed evidence that someone can start with their problem, understand the workflow, and continue without ecosystem vocabulary or Pablo.
Why it mattersDemocratization is the product's essence. If only the author can install, verify, and operate it, the essential goal remains unmet.
06

The Control Tower split is sequencing, not completion

PRD-24 validly removes Apple packaging from the framework critical path, but app publication, installation, native enforcement, crash-only supervision, and app cold-machine proof remain deferred.

What that meansPRD-23 can eventually prove a framework that works after neutral provisioning without also shipping a new app release.
Why it mattersThe original whole-ecosystem promise includes one-double-click setup and dependable supervision for a non-technical person. That promise still requires Control Tower.

Decision-ordered continuation

What must happen before “done” becomes honest

1

Complete protected review and release

Obtain independent human reviews, merge through normal protections, and publish new immutable framework, organization, and accounting identities. Do not bypass the gates.

2

Prove the released installed truth

Reinstall the reviewed identities, verify receipts and locks, retain the 19/19 round trip, remove all S0 failures, and disposition every residual instead of relying on regression stability.

3

Run the real effectiveness cases

Execute EVAL-01, EVAL-02, one of EVAL-03/EVAL-04, and EVAL-05 across foundation and applicable layered conditions, preserving criterion-level Claude/Codex evidence without a blended score.

4

Propagate once, safely

Complete pre-fan-out security, regenerate and approve the exact census, run canonical fan-out only for eligible projects, and complete post-fan-out security and conformance.

5

Prove the person and the clean environment

Run the isolated clean-home journey, then observe a non-technical participant starting with a problem and continuing without Pablo, Git, or ecosystem vocabulary.

6

Rejoin the Control Tower track for the whole goal

Finish the signed app release, installation, trustworthy supervision, cold-machine, and non-technical setup proof. Then run the final cross-record audit and close the initiative from evidence.
