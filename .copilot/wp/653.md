## Allowlist integrity and abuse review — PASS with bounded residual review duty

The allowlist requires an exact repository-relative path, exact known rule, SHA-256 of the complete normalized line, positive duplicate occurrence, substantive rationale, HTTPS URL on a narrowly accepted official agency host, and review date. Duplicate entries and unexpected fields fail validation.

Independent tamper checks altered path, rule, line hash, and occurrence separately. Each change stopped the corresponding current finding from being allowed. Rule/hash/occurrence tampering also created a stale entry; a path move was non-applicable rather than stale but still left the original source finding unapproved. There is no blanket file, directory, rule, or value exclusion.

Independent malformed-entry checks rejected:

- a path escaping the consumable skill tree;
- inadequate rationale;
- non-HTTPS evidence;
- URL user-info designed to disguise an unapproved host;
- unknown rule;
- malformed hash;
- zero occurrence;
- malformed review date.

The scanner does not cryptographically attest the truth of a rationale or continuously fetch/version official evidence. An authorized contributor could propose a same-commit source and allowlist change. This is not an unauthenticated bypass: it remains a reviewed repository change. TASK-297 must verify official-source semantics at owner approval and bind the reviewed identities into the signed immutable release. Review dates are syntactically enforced; source currency is a human/release responsibility.
